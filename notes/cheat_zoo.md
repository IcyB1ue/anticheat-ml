# Cheat zoo

| Cheat | What it changes | Signal the server can see |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Lets you hit players from farther away than normal | Distance to the target at each attack. Legit hits stay around 3 blocks or less; a cheater lands many hits at 3.5 to 4.5 blocks. |
| Killaura | On your screen, you don't turn your head but everyone that enters your "aura" gets hit | The direction the player is facing doesn't match the position of the player they hit, angles change by large amounts in a single tick, hitting someone while doing something else |
| Aim assist | Slightly tracks the enemy when aiming | The standard deviation of the amount of turn when tracking is non-human (too low) |
| Autoclicker | Clicks for you while you hold your mouse or press a keybind | The standard deviation of the gap between each click is non-human (too low) |
| Velocity | Reduces or completely removes the knockback you take | Your position after being hit changed too little |
| Fly | Lets you move in the air without falling | The y position per tick ignores gravity. A real jump rises for 6 ticks, then falls faster each tick; a flyer stays level or keeps rising, often while claiming "on ground". |
| Speed | Increases your in-game speed, allowing you to move quicker | Change in distance over time is too high relative to a normal player |
| Timer | Speeds up your game clock | Count movement packets over a long period: more than 20 per real second suggests Timer. Don't decide too quickly, because lag can make packets arrive in bursts. |
| Scaffold | Places blocks for you | Not even looking in the direction the player placed the block in |
| Macros | Repeats a task for you | Doing something repetitive where values or changes in values are too consistent |
| Bots | Plays the whole entire account for you | Repetition, plays all the time, many accounts behaving the same |
